# Editor SOP — Taking on a Project & Tracking Time in Hubstaff

A quick guide for editors: how to pick up an ad set and log your time correctly.

---

## 1. Why this matters (read once)
When you work an ad set, you **track your time in Hubstaff on specific tasks**. That time
flows into the KPI dashboard, which compares it against the *standard time* for that exact
deliverable. So two things have to be right:
1. You pick the **task that matches your deliverable AND its quantity**.
2. You **start the clock when you begin and stop it when you stop**.

Get those right and your KPIs are accurate and fair. Track on the wrong task (or leave the
clock running) and the numbers are wrong.

---

## 2. Take on the project (in the Google Sheet)
1. Open the **Testing** tab.
2. Put **your name in the `Editor` column** for the ad set you're taking.
   - This automatically opens a Discord thread for that project so the team can follow it.
3. As you work, keep the **status column** updated — it updates the Discord thread automatically.

---

## 3. Track your time in Hubstaff

### Step 1 — Open the project
In Hubstaff, find the **project** that matches your ad set. It's named after the funnel,
e.g. `NLBE565 | Bee Venom Mouthwash`.

### Step 2 — Pick the RIGHT task (match type **and** quantity)
Each project already contains every task option. Choose the one that matches **both**:
- **what** you're making (1-min video, 2-min video, image ad…), and
- **how many** you're making in this ad set.

The quantity is built into the task name — that *is* how you "select the number":

| You're making | Use this task |
|---|---|
| 3 × 2-minute videos | `Video - 2min - 3 Videos` |
| 2 × 1-minute videos | `Video – under 1 min - 2 Videos` |
| 4 × 3-minute videos | `Video - 3min - 4 Videos` |
| 12 image ads | `Image (from scratch) - 12 Image Ads` |

### Step 3 — Start / stop the clock
- **Start** the timer on that task **when you begin** working on it.
- **Stop** it when you pause, finish, switch to a different task type, or end your day.
- **Only one timer runs at a time.** When you switch deliverable types, stop one task and
  start the next.

---

## 4. Worked example
**Ad set = 3 × 2-min videos + 2 × 1-min videos**

1. ▶️ Start **`Video - 2min - 3 Videos`** → make the three 2-minute videos → ⏹️ stop when done.
2. ▶️ Start **`Video – under 1 min - 2 Videos`** → make the two 1-minute videos → ⏹️ stop when done.

**Image ad set = 12 images**
- ▶️ Start **`Image (from scratch) - 12 Image Ads`** → make them → ⏹️ stop when done.

That's it — Hubstaff now holds your real time on each deliverable, and the dashboard scores it
against the standard for "3× 2-min videos" and "2× 1-min videos".

---

## 5. Task reference — which task to pick

**Videos with a quantity (choose `… - N Videos`, N = 1–6):**
- `Video - 2min - N Videos`
- `Video - 3min - N Videos`
- `Video – 4 min - N Videos`
- `Video – under 1 min - N Videos`  ← use this for **1-minute** videos

**Longer videos (no quantity variant — use the base task per video, start/stop each):**
- `Video – 5 min` · `Video – 6 min` · `Video – 7 min`

**Other video tasks:**
- `Video – Script change` · `Video – Scrollstopper`

**Images:**
- `Image (from scratch) - N Image Ads` (N = 10–20)
- `Image (from scratch)` or `Image (AI regen)` for other/smaller batches

**Research:**
- `Deep Research GPT`

> If the exact count variant doesn't exist for your deliverable, use the closest base task
> (e.g. one `Video – 5 min` task, tracked once per video) and flag it to your lead.

---

## 6. Golden rules
- ✅ Match the task to the **exact count** in your ad set.
- ✅ **One timer at a time** — switch tasks when you switch work.
- ✅ **Always stop the clock** when you stop working (breaks, end of day, task switch).
- ✅ Keep the sheet **status** updated as you progress.
- ❌ Don't track on a generic/wrong task — it skews your KPIs.
- ❌ Don't leave the timer running on a finished task.

---

### Cheat sheet
> **Assign yourself in the sheet → open the Hubstaff project (funnel name) → start the task
> that matches your deliverable + count → stop it when you're done.**
